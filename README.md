# eHanda Pampanga

A disaster-preparedness Mock-up website for Pampanga province. Not an official Government System.

## What is the inspiration?

It started when i saw a video on tiktok by a team in eGov Hackathon 2026, i think it was called "eSalba" and it was a something something help when there are emergencies. When i was thinking of what website i will do for Midterms figma output, its already the rainy season and i decided to do this. i thought of a website that people can visit when they need information about their localities in times of disaster and calamities.
