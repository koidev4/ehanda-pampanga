Hazard Guides

General, province-wide guidance for the four hazards most relevant to Pampanga. Tap a hazard to expand,.For evacuation centers and contacts specific to your city or municipality, see the Municipalities page.

Typhoon

Pampanga is regularly in the path of typhoons during the June to November season. Watch PAGASA advisories once a storm is inside the Philippine Area of Responsibility.

Before

  • Track PAGASA typhoon bulletins and your barangays signal warnings.
  • Charge phones and power banks; store extra batteries.
  • Secure loose roofing, signage, and outdoor items.
  • Prepare the household go-bag (see Checklist page)
  • Know your family's evacuation center and the route to it.

During

  • Stay indoors, away from windows and glass doors.
  • Monitor radio or barangay announcements for updates.
  • If ordered to evacuate, leave early and do not wait for floodwater to rise.
  • Avoid using electrical appliances during heavy rain and lightning.
  • Keep children and elderly household members together in one room.

After

  • Wait for an official all clear before leaving the evacuation center.
  • Inspect your home for structural damage before re-entering.
  • Avoid downed power lines and standing floodwater.
  • Report damage and injuries to the barangay office.
  • Boil or treat water before drinking if supply may be contaminated.

Earthquake

Pampanga lies within a seismically active region of Luzon. A strong earthquake can strike with no warning, so households should know their drop, cover, and hold positions in advance.

Before

  • Identify safe spots in each room bettter if under sturdy tables, away from glass and tall shelves.
  • Agree on a family meeting point (see the Checklist page).
  • Secure heavy furniture and appliances to walls where possible.
  • Keep the go-bag and important documents accessible.
  • Learn how to shut off your household gas and electricity.

During

  • Drop, cover, and hold and get under sturdy furniture and protect your head and neck.
  • Stay indoors until the shaking stops; do not run outside mid-quake.
  • If driving, pull over away from bridges, overpasses, and power lines.
  • Do not use elevators.

After

  • Check yourself and others for injuries before moving around.
  • Expect aftershocks and stay away from damaged structures.
  • Check for gas leaks, damaged wiring, and structural cracks before re-entering buildings.
  • Use text messages rather than calls to keep phone lines open for emergencies.
  • Tune in to barangay or LGU channels for verified updates.

Flood

Flood risk varies a lot by town in Pampanga river delta and low-lying municipalities flood far more often than upland areas. Check your municipality's flood-risk note on the Municipalities page.

Before

  • Know if your barangay is in a flood-prone or low lying zone.
  • Prepare the go-bag and store important documents in a waterproof bag.
  • Identify higher ground or your barangays evacuation center in advance.
  • Keep a battery powered radio and flashlight ready.
  • Move vehicles and valuables to higher ground when flooding is forecast.

During

  • Move to higher floors or your designated evacuation center early.
  • Never wade, swim, or drive through floodwater.
  • Turn off electricity at the breaker if water is rising indoors.
  • Watch for advisories on dam or reservoir releases affecting your area.
  • Keep children away from drainage canals and rivers.

After

  • Wait for the allclear before returning home.
  • Avoid contact with floodwater because it may be contaminated.
  • Disinfect surfaces that were submerged before using them again.
  • Check for structural damage and unstable ground before entering your home.
  • Watch for displaced wildlife such as snakes in flooded areas.

Fire

Household and community fires can spread quickly in dense barangays. Most fire deaths come from smoke, not flame — knowing your exit routes matters as much as prevention.

Before

  • Install and test smoke detectors; keep a fire extinguisher accessible.
  • Avoid overloading electrical outlets or leaving cooking unattended.
  • Plan two exit routes from every room and a family meeting point outside.
  • Keep flammable materials away from stoves and open flames.
  • Know the Bureau of Fire Protection hotline for your municipality (see Municipalities page).

During

  • Get out immediately — do not stop to gather belongings.
  • Stay low to avoid smoke inhalation.
  • Once out, stay out and call the fire department from a safe distance.
  • If clothing catches fire: stop, drop, and roll

After

  • Do not re-enter the structure until officials declare it safe.
  • Contact your barangay and insurance provider (if applicable) to report damage.
  • Watch for structural weaknesses from fire and water damage.
  • Seek medical attention for any smoke inhalation, even if you feel fine.
