About

eHanda Pampanga centralizes evacuation info, emergency contacts, go-bag guidance, and hazard-specific instructions for all 22 cities and municipalities of Pampanga. Built to still work when the internet doesn't.

More

What is eHanda?

eHanda Pampanga is an academic mock-up website: a province-wide disaster-preparedness reference organized so any resident can find their own city or municipality and get hazard specific, printable guidance in a few taps.

Evacuation centers Click here. contact numbers, and local risk notes throughout the site are sample data for demonstration purposes and are clearly marked as such. To report an outdated detail or to volunteer with barangay disaster response, contact your Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committee.

Every page on this site is printable on its own. During a typhoon or earthquake, a printed copy of a hazard guide, checklist, or contact sheet may be the only version a resident can access, so print layout is treated as a core feature here, not decoration.
